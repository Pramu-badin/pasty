Our Sweet Story

Hot Pasty Bunny started as a small home kitchen dream in 2020. What began as baking treats for neighbors quickly grew into a beloved local bakery, all thanks to our commitment to quality and that extra dash of love in every recipe.

Named after our mascot bunny who always seemed to appear when fresh pastries were cooling, we've kept that same warmth and whimsy in everything we do.
